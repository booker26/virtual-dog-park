Dog Encyclopedia

Three breeds to get you started.

Beagle

  • Size: Small to medium. Usually 13–15 inches tall and about 20–30 pounds.
  • Personality: Happy, curious, and friendly. They love to follow their nose!
  • Everyday care: Needs daily walks and play. Their floppy ears should be checked and cleaned often.

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Medium to large. About 21–24 inches tall and 55–75 pounds.
  • Personality: Gentle, smart, and eager to please. Great with kids and families.
  • Everyday care: Loves exercise and games like fetch. Brush the golden coat a few times a week.

Poodle

  • Size: Comes in three sizes: Toy, Miniature, and Standard. Standard is about 15+ inches tall.
  • Personality: Very smart and active. Learns tricks quickly and enjoys being busy.
  • Everyday care: Curly coat needs regular grooming. Daily play and puzzle toys keep them happy.
